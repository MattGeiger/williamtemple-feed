## Rollback

Retain the healthy 1.8.1 images and the matching pre-upgrade snapshot above.
Prefer a forward correction for the additive nullable-column migration.
A full rollback must stop writers, restore the matching database/storage pair
and Compose environment, then recreate backend/frontend at the recorded 1.8.1
identities. Restoring the pre-upgrade database discards subsequent writes and
needs a deliberate operator decision. Contract-13 logical backups cannot be
restored into the older reader. Do not seed or replace production data as part
of this upgrade.
