# Inventory

Inventory is where FEED tracks pantry food categories, food items, limits, and stock status.

## Who Uses Inventory?

Inventory is mainly for Social Services staff who keep food lists accurate for clients and volunteers.

## Why Limits Matter

Limits help the pantry share high-demand food fairly.

A limit is not just a note. It guides what appears on shopping lists and helps control how quickly limited items run out.

## Categories

Categories group food items, such as Produce, Dairy, or Canned Goods.

Use Categories when you need to:

- Add a new kind of food group.
- Rename a category.
- Change the category limit.
- Choose the icon shown for that category.

## Food Items

Food Items are the individual foods clients may choose.

Use Food Items when you need to:

- Add a new item.
- Mark an item in stock or out of stock.
- Mark an item as clearance or limited supply.
- Set an item limit.
- Add dietary labels like vegan, halal, kosher, or gluten free.

### Logistics Information

Use the Logistics tab when adding or editing a Food Item to record:

- **Purchase Price**: blank means Unknown, $0.00 means Donated/Free, and a positive amount means Purchased.
- **Units per Purchase**: the number of base units in the purchased package. One means Each.
- **Estimated Quantity**: a whole-number estimate of the base units currently on hand.

FEED uses quantity changes to estimate burn rates. Marking an item Out of Stock records its estimated quantity as zero.

## How To Update Stock Status

The Food Items table shows each item, its category, stock status, limit, and row action menu.

![Food Items table with stock status, category filters, and row actions](/help-screenshots/inventory-food-items-table.png)

1. Open Food Items.
2. Search or filter to find the item.
3. Open the item action menu.
4. Choose the update you need.
5. Check that the table shows the new status.

## When To Edit A Category Instead

Edit the category when the rule applies to a whole group of food.

Edit the food item when the rule applies only to one food.

## What To Check Before You Delete

Before deleting a category or food item, make sure it is not still used by current shopping lists, staff workflows, or translations.
